协议解析窗口为实时曲线留出了更多空间。

- 左侧「数据面板」默认收窄，可拖动分隔条调整宽度。收起后曲线占满窗口，继续采集并显示图例。
- 单模板时显示设备地址，多模板时同时显示模板名。悬停在当前值上可查看更新时间，拉宽面板可显示时间列。「编辑协议」现在位于模板列表旁。
- 未开始绘图时，曲线区会提示等待数据或勾选字段。多设备曲线默认用线条显示，重叠的波形更容易区分。

## English

The protocol analysis window gives live plots more room.

- The left **Data panel** starts narrower. Drag the divider to resize it. Hiding the panel gives the plot the full width and shows a legend while capture continues.
- With one template selected, the list shows device addresses. With multiple templates, it also shows their names. Hover over a reading for its update time, or widen the panel to see the time column. **Edit protocol** now sits beside the template list.
- When there is no plot yet, the chart area shows the next step. The default line display makes overlapping device curves easier to compare.
